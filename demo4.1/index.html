    Study Recommendation

    Revise Photosynthesis

    Biology · Cell Structure & Energy Transfer (8 min sprint)

    1. Light-Dependent Reactions (Thylakoid Membrane)

    Photons strike chlorophyll molecules in Photosystem II, exciting electrons and splitting water into oxygen, protons, and electrons. The electron transport chain pumps protons into the lumen, generating ATP and NADPH.

    2. Light-Independent Reactions / Calvin Cycle (Stroma)

    RuBisCO fixes carbon dioxide onto RuBP (Ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate). Using ATP and NADPH from light reactions, 3-PGA is reduced to G3P to synthesize glucose.
