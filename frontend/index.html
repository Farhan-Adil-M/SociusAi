How it works

  1. 1

    Add the material you have

    Drop in a PDF or PowerPoint, or paste a web page. Socius extracts the text, keeps its page and slide positions, and makes it searchable.

  2. 2

    Ask, then check the answer

    Every answer shows the file, the page, and the exact passage it came from. Open the citation and read the source yourself.

  3. 3

    Practise until it sticks

    Quizzes and flashcards are built from the same material. Your score decides what you see next — no guesswork, no magic.

What you get

Answers with receipts

Retrieval finds the relevant passages first, so the answer is written from your material — and the citation tells you exactly where to look.

Quizzes you can't peek at

Four-choice questions generated per topic. The answer key stays on the server until you submit, so nothing leaks before you commit.

Flashcards in one click

Turn a topic into a deck of question-and-answer cards, flip through them, and mark what you know so you can see what is left.

Mastery you can measure

Your progress is a plain ratio: correct answers over attempts. It survives restarts and never quietly rounds itself up.

The difficulty follows you

Miss too many questions and the next set eases off. Clear a topic and it gets harder. The rule is written down, not learned behind closed doors.

  • under 50% mastery → easy questions
  • 50–79% mastery → medium questions
  • 80% and up mastery → hard questions
